# Working rules for this repository

These are the owner's standing rules. Follow them in every session.

1. **Branches:** before creating any branch, ask the owner for its name first. Never choose one yourself.
2. **Dependabot:** pull requests from Dependabot are assigned to the owner (`MrZoZ`), who also reviews them. Do not leave them to Claude or to a bot. Claude only helps when the owner asks.
3. **Commits:** keep messages short and simple, and do not say who wrote them.
4. **Merging:** do not merge a pull request with failing checks.
